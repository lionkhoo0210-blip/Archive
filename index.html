THE INSPIRATION

If you're wondering why there are so many Minecraft references...

Well, here's your answer.

Unstable SMP

Wifies

The Search for Leviathan

Minecraft Live Decoding Website

wifies.net/mclive

His decoding website for Minecraft Live was also one of the main inspirations behind the Archive.

I really liked the idea of turning a website into an actual mystery instead of just a normal webpage.

So I thought, why not make my own version, with my own puzzles and ideas?

And somehow... it turned into all of this.

I've always found Minecraft mysteries really interesting.

The hidden details, the storytelling, and the feeling of discovering something that was never meant to be found.

So I thought...

Why not create something like that myself?

THE FORGOTTEN LIBRARY

This entire library started with one picture.

Original reference found online. Watermark: Rana Rosso.

I saw this picture and thought, "Yeah, I could probably build that."

Turns out, building it was not quite that simple.

Bookshelves.

Pillars.

Lighting.

More bookshelves.

Even more bookshelves.

But eventually, it became the Forgotten Library.

And of course, I couldn't just leave it as a normal library.

Where's the fun in that?

So I filled it with hidden messages, mysterious books and secrets for you to discover.

If you haven't explored it yet, you definitely should.
